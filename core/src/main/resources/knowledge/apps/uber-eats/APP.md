## What it is
Uber Eats is a food-delivery app, package `com.ubercab.eats`.

## Interface
Home / search, restaurant menus, Cart and Orders. Food delivery and Grocery, Convenience, Retail and the like are separate channels.

## Features
- **Restaurants**: viewed per delivery address, showing name, rating, reviews, delivery estimate, delivery fee, offers, open status and link, with Delivery and Pickup modes. A closed restaurant shows no delivery estimate.
- **Menu**: dish names, categories, prices in the original currency, options, sold-out state. Usually viewable without signing in.
- **Cart**: shows the chosen options, quantities, order minimum, each fee, address and door details, and the total. One Add tap adds one item.
- **Orders**: Orders holds past orders and the current order's delivery progress.

## Known behaviour
- Restaurants and supermarket retail items belong to different channels.
- A sign-in or verification may appear.
