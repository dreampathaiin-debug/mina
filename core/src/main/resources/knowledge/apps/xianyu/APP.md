## What it is
Goofish is a second-hand marketplace app, package `com.taobao.idlefish`.

## Features
- **Goods and rentals**: besides second-hand goods there are personal rentals and sublets. The original description, asking price, deposit and payment terms, utility rates and meter reading, and pet restrictions are readable. A sign-in is usually required. Prices and claims like "the landlord themselves" are written by the poster.
- **Message the seller**: "Chat" on a product page opens a conversation with the seller, where messages can be sent and replies read. After sending, the message appears in the conversation.
