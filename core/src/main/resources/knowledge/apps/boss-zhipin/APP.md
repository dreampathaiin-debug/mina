## What it is
BOSS Zhipin is a jobs and hiring app, package `com.hpbr.bosszhipin`.

## Known behaviour
- When signed out, opening lands on the sign-in page: "Sign in with WeChat", "One-tap sign in", "Other ways to sign in", with agreeing to the user agreement and privacy policy required below.
- Back at the top left of the sign-in page enters the phone-number sign-in page (phone number, "Next", "Not receiving SMS", plus WeChat, QQ and other ways to sign in); pressing back again exits the app. There is no browse-without-signing-in entry.
