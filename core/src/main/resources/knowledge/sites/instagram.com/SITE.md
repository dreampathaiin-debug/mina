## What it is
An image and short-video social platform, with profiles, a feed, search and explore, posts, Reels and direct messages.

## Pages and URLs
Home `https://www.instagram.com/`. A profile is `/<username>/`.

## Features
- **Profile**: username, bio, post count, follower count, following count, verification mark and private state. Display names can repeat; the username is the unique one.
- **Posts and Reels**: author, caption, publish time, like and comment numbers. Entered from searching an account, a hashtag or a post link.

## Known behaviour
Private accounts and rate-limited pages both show as if empty.
