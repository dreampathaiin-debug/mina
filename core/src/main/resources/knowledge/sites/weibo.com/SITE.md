## What it is
A public social platform; content is users' short posts with repost, comment and like counts. Recent events and user complaints can be searched.

## Pages and URLs
- Search: `https://s.weibo.com/weibo?q=<encoded keyword>&xsort=hot`, with `xsort=hot` sorting by heat.
- Date window: add `timescope=custom:YYYY-MM-DD:YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Features
- **Search**: result cards carry author, publish time, body and repost/comment/like counts.
- **Original posts**: open one for the full original text, author and counts. Reposts can be traced to the original publisher.
- **Phone app**: opening it lands on the current screen; see `weibo` for the matching description.

## Known behaviour
- The front of a heat-ranked search often holds marketing accounts and reposts.
- Repost and like counts show spread only, not the number of people with the same experience.
