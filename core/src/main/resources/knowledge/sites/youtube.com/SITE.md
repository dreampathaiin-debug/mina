## What it is
A video platform.

## Pages and URLs
- Search: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<encoded term>`.
- Video: `/watch?v=<id>`; channels use channel links.

## Features
- **Videos**: search results carry title, channel, duration, view count and publish time. On a video page the description expands, and scrolling loads comments. Public videos need no sign-in.
- **Channels**: name, subscriber count, an About blurb and the latest videos.
- **Subtitles**: videos with subtitles yield their subtitle text; a long video's subtitles run long.

## Known behaviour
- View and subscriber counts often display abbreviated ("12k", "1.2M"); exact numbers need the details.
- Private, members-only, age-restricted or region-locked videos show no content, which is different from "no search results".
- Unavailable subtitles may mean the video has none, is restricted, or a network problem.
